# Conceptual Masterplan Overview
**Project:** Instituto Vida Plena — Life in Fullness Institute

---

## 8 Functional Zones
1. **Welcome & Administration:** Main gate, reception, family consultation room, administrative offices.
2. **Residential Wings (Ala Vida & Ala Plena):** Accessible suites, private lounges, and support stations.
3. **Health & Care:** 24/7 nursing station, medication room, doctor's office, physical therapy clinic.
4. **Community & Memory:** Living lounge, library, Memory Workshop, and Audio-Video Recording Studio.
5. **Spirituality & Peace:** Christian chapel, quiet prayer room, and contemplative garden.
6. **Dining & Culinary:** Commercial kitchen, cold storage, sunlit dining hall, and Café Vida Plena.
7. **Services & Support:** Barrier laundry, waste central, maintenance, emergency diesel generator.
8. **Gardens & Community:** Central Garden, Memory Garden, Sensory Garden, raised therapeutic vegetable beds, Family Plaza.

---

> **Notice:** The Masterplan is a conceptual spatial study intended for architectural guidance and does not represent an approved technical blueprint.
