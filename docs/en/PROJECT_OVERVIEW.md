# Project Overview: Instituto Vida Plena
**Date:** July 28, 2026  
**Status:** [Conceptual & Planning Stage]

---

## Executive Summary
**Instituto Vida Plena — Life in Fullness Institute** is a Christian-inspired social initiative dedicated to creating a dignified, warm, and comprehensive residential home for senior citizens in Macapá, Amapá, Northern Brazil.

Unlike cold institutional facilities, Instituto Vida Plena is designed as a single-story, tropical boutique-style senior living community centered around a lush **Central Garden**. The initial conceptual capacity is planned for **24 residents** `[Sizing hypothesis under feasibility review]`.

---

## Core Pillars
1. **Dignified Care:** 24/7 nursing, physical therapy, and personalized nutrition in a cozy residential environment.
2. **Biophilic Architecture:** Single-story accessible design (ABNT NBR 9050) with natural light, cross-ventilation, and shaded verandas adapted to Macapá's tropical climate.
3. **Memory Preservation:** Dedicated Memory Workshop, Recording Studio, and Memory Garden to honor each resident's life story.
4. **Christian Values & Respect:** Rooted in Christian love, compassion, and transparency, while offering unconditional respect and warm welcome to individuals of all beliefs.
